About Me

I am an undergraduate student at California State Polytechnic University, Humboldt, pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Political Science, Law and Policy. My academic interests center on the relationship between political institutions, legal frameworks, public policy, and international affairs.

My educational background also includes an Associate of Arts in Criminal Justice from Diablo Valley College. This foundation helped develop my interest in legal systems and the institutions responsible for interpreting and applying the law.

As I approach graduation in 2027, I am looking forward to pursuing graduate education and continuing my academic development in international law.
