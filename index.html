About Me

An academic journey centered on political institutions, legal systems, and the principles that shape society.

I am Cody Justin Müders, an undergraduate student pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Political Science, Law and Policy at California State Polytechnic University, Humboldt, with an anticipated graduation in 2027.

My academic interests focus on the relationship between law, political institutions, public policy, and the broader international community. I am particularly interested in understanding how legal frameworks influence governance, international relations, and the development of public institutions.

Following my undergraduate studies, I look forward to pursuing graduate education, with a particular interest in International Law.
